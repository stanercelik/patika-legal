Support

If you may be in immediate danger, contact your local emergency number now. Patika is not an emergency service. The in-app support route is always free.

Contact

For help with access, purchases, account linking, data export or account deletion, email tanercelik2001@gmail.com. If it is about a purchase, include the purchase date and, if you have it, the App Store order ID. Please do not send what you wrote in the app, your check-in answers or other sensitive details by email.

Restore purchases

On the same Patika account, tap Restore purchases on the purchase screen. One-time purchases do not reliably restore from the App Store receipt on a different device; linking Sign in with Apple or Google keeps your path’s access. If access has not appeared after paying, wait a moment for verification, then contact us.

Refunds

Request App Store refunds from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. When a refund is confirmed, paid access to that path is updated. Your notes and check-ins stay available.

Your data

Export your data or delete your account from the Me tab. See the Privacy Policy for details.
